Delete your Dust Rider account

Dust Rider is a game by Baoogames. You can delete your account and all its data at any time.

In the game (immediate)

  1. Open Dust Rider.
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, then tap it again to confirm.

Your account, profile, coins and their history, achievements and online duels are deleted at once.

By email

If you no longer have the game, write to contact@baoogames.com from the email of your Google or Apple account, with the subject "Delete account". We will delete it within the legal time limits and let you know.

Guest accounts: they have no email, so we cannot tell which one is yours. Delete it from the game; if you no longer have it, it is deleted automatically after 12 months without use.

More details in the privacy policy.
