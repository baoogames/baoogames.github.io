# Privacy policy — Double Getaway

**Last updated:** November 6, 2026

## 1. Who is responsible

The people responsible for your personal data are **Oscar Fabián Ortega Guerrero** and **Brayan Andrés Viveros Melo**, individuals acting as joint controllers, who make the game under the name **Baoo Games** ("we"), based in San Juan de Pasto, Colombia. Oscar Fabián Ortega Guerrero publishes the game on the App Store and Google Play. Baoo Games is not yet an incorporated company.

Contact for any question or request about your data: **contact@baoogames.com**.

## 2. What data we process

**a) Data that stays on your phone.** Your language, music, sound effects, vibration, lives and your choice about ads are stored only on your phone. We don't receive them.

**b) Your account.** So you never lose your progress, a **guest account** is created the first time you open the game: an anonymous account with only a random identifier, no name and no email. If you want, you can sign in with your **Apple** or **Google** account from **Settings › Account**. In that case we receive that account's unique identifier, your **email address** (or the private address Apple creates if you choose to hide it), your **name** and, with Google, the address of your **profile picture**. We don't show them to anyone.

**c) One account for all our games.** Your account is the same in every Baoo Games game (for example, Dust Rider): if you sign in with the same Apple or Google account, we recognize you in each one. What you save in each game is kept apart, tagged with the game it belongs to, and one game can't see another's data.

**d) Your progress in the game.** For each level you beat: your **stars** and your **fewest moves**. Also how many **hints** you have left, the **last level** you reached, and when you first and last played. That way you can pick up where you left off on another phone.

**e) Data collected by ads.** Double Getaway shows ads: reward videos, always optional (a life, hints or extra moves), and now and then a full-screen ad between levels. They are shown by **Unity LevelPlay** together with the ad networks connected to that service (today, among others, **ironSource**, **Google AdMob** and **Unity Ads**). These providers may collect:
- your device's advertising identifier (Android: advertising ID; iOS: IDFA, only if you allow it in the system prompt),
- your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
- device data (model, system version, language, network),
- your interactions with the ads (whether they were shown, watched or tapped).

If you choose **"Basic only"** on the privacy screen, this data is not used to personalize ads.

**f) Technical data.** Like any online service, our server provider receives your IP address and technical data from each connection.

**g) What we don't collect.** We don't collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or sensitive data. The game has no chat and no online play with other people, and it uses no analytics tools.

## 3. What we use your data for

- Running the game: your account and saving your progress so you don't lose it.
- Showing ads, which is what funds the game.
- Protecting the game and preventing fraud and abuse.
- Answering your questions and requests.

## 4. Legal basis

- **Account and progress:** necessary to provide the service you asked for.
- **Personalized ads:** your consent, which you give or refuse on the privacy screen. You can change it any time in **Settings › Ads**.
- **Security:** our legitimate interest in protecting the game and its players.

## 5. Who we share data with

- **Supabase**, which stores your account and progress on our behalf. The data is stored in the **United States** (Oregon).
- **Apple**, if you use "Continue with Apple".
- **Google**, if you use "Continue with Google", and as one of the ad networks (AdMob).
- The **ad providers** listed in 2.e.
- Authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data ourselves.

## 6. International transfers

Your account data is stored in the United States, and some providers may process data in other countries. We choose providers that protect data, but the laws of those countries may differ from yours.

## 7. How long we keep it

- Data on your phone, until you uninstall the game.
- Your account data, while the account exists. You can delete just this game's data or the whole account (section 9).
- **Guest accounts that go unused** for **12 months** (no one opens any of our games with them) are deleted automatically, with everything saved in them. Apple or Google accounts are not deleted for inactivity: only you can delete them.
- Ad data, according to each provider's policies.

## 8. Your rights

You can **access, update and correct** your data, **ask for it to be deleted**, **withdraw your consent** and **access it free of charge**. You can also file a complaint with the data protection authority of your country (in Colombia, the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio).

To exercise these rights, write to **contact@baoogames.com**. We will answer within the time limits set by law.

You can also limit ad tracking in your phone's settings and change your choice about ads inside the game.

## 9. Deleting your data or your account

If you signed in with Apple or Google, open **Settings › Account** in the game and choose:
- **"Delete progress in this game":** deletes your Double Getaway levels, stars and saved data, on your phone and on our server. Your account stays for our other games.
- **"Delete Baoo Games account":** deletes your whole account and everything saved with it **in all our games**. This can't be undone.

You can also ask for it by writing to **contact@baoogames.com** from the email linked to your account, with the subject "Delete account"; we will do it within the legal time limits. A **guest** account has no email, so we can't tell which one is yours: if you didn't sign in with Apple or Google, once you uninstall the game your guest account is deleted automatically after the period in section 7.

This information is also at **https://baoogames.com/double-getaway/delete-account/**.

## 10. Children

Double Getaway is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children under that age. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

## 11. Security

We apply reasonable measures to protect data, such as encrypted connections and database access rules so that each player can only read their own data, but no measure is foolproof.

## 12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. If the change affects how we use your data for ads, we will ask for your consent again.
