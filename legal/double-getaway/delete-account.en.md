# Delete your Double Getaway data or account

Your account is the same in every **Baoo Games** game. That's why you can choose between deleting only Double Getaway's data or the whole account.

## In the game

If you signed in with Apple or Google, open **Settings › Account** and choose:

1. **Delete progress in this game:** deletes your Double Getaway levels, stars and saved data, on your phone and on our server. Your account keeps working in our other games.
2. **Delete Baoo Games account:** deletes your account and everything saved with it **in all our games** (for example, your Dust Rider data too). This can't be undone.

Confirm, and the game goes back to the start screen as a new player.

## By email

Write to **contact@baoogames.com** from the email linked to your Apple or Google account, with the subject "Delete account", and tell us whether you want to delete only Double Getaway or the whole account. We will do it within the legal time limits.

## If you play as a guest

A guest account has no email, so we can't tell which one is yours. If you didn't sign in with Apple or Google, once you uninstall the game your guest account is deleted automatically after **12 months** without being used in any of our games.

## What is deleted

Your progress (stars, moves, hints, last level) and, if you delete the whole account, also your profile and everything from our other games. More details in the [privacy policy](../privacy/).
